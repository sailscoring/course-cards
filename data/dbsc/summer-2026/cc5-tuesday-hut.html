Card notes

Marks in red (p) to be rounded to Port, except X to be passed to Port. Marks in green (s) to be rounded to Starboard.

Start line is formed by a transit above the hut, with limit mark orange, black, green or yellow, as displayed on the hut.

Finish line is between an orange line on the corner of the hut & the orange limit mark. Restrictions around hut - see SI H5.10.
