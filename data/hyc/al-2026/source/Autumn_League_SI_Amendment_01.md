# Autumn League 2026, sailing instructions amendment 01

Autumn League 2026

Organised by Howth Yacht Club CLG

Saturday September 12th to Saturday October 17th

## SAILING INSTRUCTIONS

## AMENDMENT 01

Sailing instruction 6.2 D is changed to read as follows:

D Finishing Line

Round the Cans Races

From the last mark on the displayed course, boats shall sail to the finish line passing the Spit mark to starboard. The finishing line, unless a race is shortened, shall be in the vicinity of the Spit Mark (S) and the South Rowan Buoy (R) and shall be between a spherical orange Mark F and the main mast (or red/white pole) on the adjacent Committee Finishing Vessel displaying a blue flag.
