# Autumn League 2025, change to sailing instructions no. 2

Autumn League 2025

Organised by Howth Yacht Club CLG

Change to Sailing Instructions No: 2

(NOTE: This change is to give greater clarity for the location of the Finisher’s Hut)

The following Sailing Instructions and Change to Sailing Instructions N0:1 are changed:

## 6.1. D Finish Line (Offshore Fleets)

The last paragraph is changed to read:

For classes 4 and 5, on two race days, when the other cruiser classes are sailing W/L courses, the finish line will be in Howth Sound. Boats shall leave the Spit Mark to Starboard, the South Rowan to Port and finish in an Easterly direction on the HYC Finish Line. The Finish line will be between a vertical line on the front of the Finisher's Hut and a red buoy. The Finisher's Hut is located on the wall of the East Pier approximately

## 6.2. D Finish Line (Inshore Fleets)

SI 6.2. D Finish Line is changed by the addition of the following:

For HYC 17 class on two race days, when the other one design classes are sailing W/L courses, the finish line will be in Howth Sound. Boats shall leave the Spit Mark to Starboard, the South Rowan to Port and finish in an Easterly direction on the HYC Finish Line. The Finish line will be between a vertical line on the front of the Finisher’s Hut and a red buoy. The Finisher's Hut is located on the wall of the East Pier approximately
