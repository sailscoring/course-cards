# Dublin Port Company Notices to Mariners 2026, summary of notices

Summary of Notices 2026

All Masters, Warranted Pilots, holders of Pilotage Exemption Certificates and other mariners in charge of vessels are hereby notified that all 2025 Notices to Mariners not previously cancelled are automatically cancelled at 23:59 hours on the 31 December 2025. At that time all 2026 Notices to Mariners will become current and will remain so unless specifically cancelled or until 23:59 hours on the 31 December 2026. Notices issued subsequently will be current from the date of issue until cancelled or until 23:59 hours on the 31st December 2026.
