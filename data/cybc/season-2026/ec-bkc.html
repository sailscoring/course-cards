Start line

SLStart lineThe start Line: shall be between the mainmast of the committee boat and an Outer Pin-end mark, laid on port-side of the committee boat. Restrictions: An inner-limit mark will be laid close to the committee boat; no boat shall pass between the inner limit buoy and the committee boat. Any boat passing between will be (DSQ) disqualified from the Championship. This inner-limit mark may be in front or behind the start line, OR leeward of the committee boat and does not form part of the start-line.

8.1

The finish line will be the same line as the start line (unless race is shortened), It will be from the main- mast on the committee boat and a pin-end mark. Inner limit mark must be observed.

12.2

START-FINISH LINE & 1st LEG : the start-finish line will be laid downwind of the first mark of the course, providing a beat for the first-leg.

12.3

Notification of Courses: Courses will be displayed on the side of the Committee boat before the race- countdown is started. (This changes rule 27.1). The RO may also announce the race-course on the VHF.
