Courses

This card lists no courses: the race officer calls the course on the day, as a sequence of the marks below, and the club's instructions on how are in the notes.

Start line

SLStart lineThe starting line is between a red and white pole on the committee boat at the one end and the course side of the starting mark at the other. An inner-distance mark may be laid. Boats shall not sail between this mark and the committee boat at any time.

Calves Week 2026 sailing instructions 12.1

Finish

FLFinish lineThe finishing line is between the red and white pole on the finishing vessel displaying a blue flag and the course side of a laid mark, or Mark No. 6 as indicated.

A course called on the day ends at FL. Calves Week 2026 sailing instructions 13.1.

Racing area

9.1 The racing area is Long Island Bay and the Fastnet Rock. All races will Start near Copper Point or Inside Harbour, and finish inside Schull Harbour.

Courses

10.1 Courses will be determined by the Race Committee using fixed marks (other than Start/Finish marks) and islands in Long Island Bay as well as the Fastnet Rock & displayed on a board on the Committee Boat on the day of the race.

10.2 Course details may be announced on VHF channel 69 prior to the start of each class.

Marks

11.1 A chart showing the positions of the permanently laid marks is available on registration.
