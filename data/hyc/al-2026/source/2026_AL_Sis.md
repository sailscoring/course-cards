# Autumn League 2026 sailing instructions

Autumn League 2026 Organised by Howth Yacht Club CLG

Saturday September 12th to Saturday October 17th

Sailing Instructions

The notation [DP] in a rule means that the penalty for a breach of that rule may, at the discretion of the Protest Committee, be less than disqualification. The notation [NP] in a rule means that a boat may not protest another boat for a breach of this rule. This changes RRS 60.1(a)

## 1. Rules

1.1 The races shall be governed by the current Racing Rules of Sailing (Rules), Irish Sailing Prescriptions, the Notice of Race and these Sailing Instructions (SI) and any changes thereto.

1.2 Competitors in races for One Design classes shall comply with their relevant Class Rules. Competitors in IRC and HPH handicap classes shall comply with the Rules of those handicapping systems

1.3 Boats competing in Class 4 and Class 5, in both IRC and HPH divisions, shall be subject to the following headsail Rule: Boats shall use only one headsail at any time (i.e. drop one headsail before setting another). This changes Rule 50.1.

## 2. Measurement

A measurer(s) competent in the measurement of boats in each one design Class and handicap system may be appointed by the Organising Authority to the Race Committee. The Race Committee may order measurement checks on any boat which enters and comes to the race area at any time during the event. A boat which the Race Committee requires to be measured shall be present at a time and place specified by the Race Committee.

## 3. Notice to Competitors

Notices to competitors will be posted on the Official Notice Board located in the archway beside the Marina Office. In addition, notifications may be made via the regatta WhatsApp group. Failure to receive such notifications will not be a case for redress.

## 4. Changes to Sailing Instructions

Changes to Sailing Instructions will be posted not later than two hours before the scheduled start of the first race so affected. Flag L displayed on the flagpole at the top of the marina bridge denotes that a change of Sailing Instructions has been made.

## 5. Signals Made Ashore

Signals made ashore will be displayed on the flagpole at the top of the marina bridge. When flag AP is displayed ashore ‘one minute’ is replaced with “not less than 45 minutes” in race signal AP. This changes the Rule ‘Race Signals’.

6.5 The Starting Time Limit shall be 5 minutes. Any boat failing to start within that time will be scored as DNS. This changes Rules 35, A4 and A5.

## 7. [DP] Re-crossing Start/Finish Lines

All boats shall keep clear of the starting lines until their Warning Signal.

Boats, having started and cleared their starting line, shall not re-cross that line whilst starts are in progress. Boats shall not cross any other starting line whilst starts are in progress. Boats shall not cross the finish line on another course.

## 8. Finishing Time Limit

The Time Limit for races on days with a single race is 1600. This will apply to HYC 17s and Classes 4 and 5 on two race days when they only have one race. In the event of a postponement and/or general recall the time limit for races so affected shall be extended by the time the start is delayed. If a boat finishes a race within this limit, the time limit for her Class shall be extended by 15 minutes in that race. A boat which fails to finish within the time limit will be scored DNF. This changes Rule 35 and A5.1. On days when a second race is sailed, the time limit for each race shall be 15 minutes from the time of the first finisher in each Class.

## 9. Hearing Requests

Hearing Requests and Scoring Inquiries should be submitted online, within the protest time limit, at https://tinyurl.com/54jfd2xs

The Protest Time Limit for the Inshore fleet is 60 minutes from the time of finish of the last boat in the last race sailed on the day by a Class assigned to the Inshore fleet. The protest time limit for the Offshore fleet is 60 minutes from the time of the finish of the last boat in the last race sailed on the day by a Class assigned to the Offshore fleet.

Notices of protests by the Race Committee will be posted at https://tinyurl.com/54jfd2xs This changes Rule 61.1(b).

The protest flag exemption for boats less than six metres shall not apply. This changes Rule 61.1(a) (2).

## 10. Protest Hearings

Protest hearings will be held on the Tuesday following the race in question at 1930 in Howth Yacht Club. On the last scheduled day of racing protests will be held on that day.

The schedule of protest hearings will be posted online, 30 minutes after the protest time limit, at https://tinyurl.com/54jfd2xs

The listing of a boat on this schedule shall constitute informing her of a hearing under Rule 63.2. Parties involved shall be present with their witnesses at the time and place indicated on the schedule.

On the last scheduled day of racing a request to reopen a hearing shall be delivered to the Protest Committee not later than 30 minutes after the party was informed of the decision. This changes Rule 66.

## 11. Scoring

The Low Point scoring system of Appendix A will apply. However, RRS A 5.2 is changed such that RRS Appendix 5.3 will apply for the series. When four or more races have been completed, a boat’s series score will be the total of her race scores excluding her worst excludable score

## 12. Navigation Marks (IALA) in Howth Sound

Rowan Rocks east cardinal mark: To be passed to the east and south Howth Bouy: To be passed to the south South Rowan Bouy: To be passed to the west and south

13 Radio Communications Competitors are advised to keep a listening watch for their respective Committee Vessel - VHF Channel 77 for Offshore Committee Vessel, Channel 68 for Inshore Committee Vessel - where information relevant to racing may be broadcast. Should this information be broadcast, it shall not constitute outside assistance and shall not be the subject of any protest or claim for redress. Failure to broadcast this information shall not be the basis for any claim for redress. Competitors who retire from a race shall inform the Race Committee promptly by radio on Channel 77 or 68, as appropriate. Competitors without radio should inform the Race Office on coming ashore.

14 Safety Competitors participate entirely at their own risk, see Fundamental Rule 3 ‘Decision to Race”’. Open boat entrants are referred to relevant Class Regulations. The wearing of suitable personal floatation devices in all boats is strongly recommended at all times.

The responsibility for the safety of competing boats and their crews lies solely with the said competitors. Howth Yacht Club, and/or the Organising Committee will not accept any liability for material damage, personal injury or death sustained in conjunction with, prior to, during or after racing.

15 Insurance Each participating boat shall be insured with adequate, valid, third-party liability insurance.

## GENERAL

When entering/leaving Howth Harbour, stay on the right-hand side of the fairway and harbour entrance i.e. always pass port to port. Also, Howth Sound is a restricted channel and accordingly boats shall not impede trawlers.

The attention of all participants is drawn to the car parking regulations in the vicinity of the Club. These are rigidly enforced by the Harbour Authority.

Competitors are granted temporary membership of Howth Yacht Club on race days of the Autumn League, subject only to the provisions of the Club Rules for withdrawing or withholding said membership.
