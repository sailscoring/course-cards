General Regulations SI 1 STANDARD TIME

1.1 All times are in Irish Summer Time (IST), which is UTC + 1 hour.

## SI 2 COMPETITOR COMMUNICATIONS

2.1 The Race Headquarters (‘RHQ’) will be located at the Kinsale Yacht Club( KYC), Kinsale, Co. Cork and will be open from 1600hrs to 1830hrs on Tuesday 24th June and 0900hrs to 1200hrs on Weds.25th June. From Thursday to Saturday, it will be open from 0830hrs to 0915hrs and 1600hrs to 1800hrs daily.

2.2 The Official Notice Board (‘ONB’) will be online at Sovereign’s Cup Official Notice Board - Kinsale Yacht Club.

2.3 All changes to the Sailing Instructions (‘SIs’) will be posted on the ONB. Any change will be posted before 0900hrs on the day it will take effect, except that any change in the schedule of races will be posted by 1900hrs on the day before such change is to take effect. A numeral pennant will be displayed under flag ‘L’ indicating the latest amendment in force.

2.4 Signals ashore will be flown from the KYC flagstaff which is located at the Eastern end of the KYC clubhouse grounds.

2.5 All competing boats shall carry a VHF Radio Transceiver.

2.6 When possible, any Sovereign’s Cup event flags provided by the OA should be flown by competing boats when berthed on the marina or when travelling to/from the racecourse.

## SI 3 RULES

3.1 The event will be governed by the Rules as defined in The Racing Rules of Sailing (RRS) including Appendix T, the prescriptions of Irish Sailing, the IRC Rule and the ECHO Progressive Rule.

3.2 The notation [NP] in a rule means that a boat may not protest another boat for breaking that rule. The Jury may refuse to hold a hearing when a boat protests based on these rules. This changes RRS 60.1 and 63.2(a).

## SI 4 REGISTRATION

4.1 For each competing boat, completion of Registration means: An entry form has been submitted and the relevant entry fee paid in full. A current valid IRC / ECHO certificate is available on the Irish Sailing/ RORC website. The boat is in full compliance with the requirements of safety or measurement checks.

## SI 5 PENALTIES

5.1 For all classes – A scoring penalty will be applied instead of turns penalties. The scoring penalty is 2 points. This amends RRS 44.3 (c).
