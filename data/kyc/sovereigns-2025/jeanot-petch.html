Supplementary sailing instructions

Supplementary Sailing Instructions for

Jeanot Petch Sovereign’s Cup Races Thursday 26 June 2025

Boats are divided into the following Groups:

Group 1: Spinnaker Classes 0 & 1, Coastal Class 1 FG 10.25

Group 2: Spinnaker Classes 2 & 3, Coastal Class 2, Non-Spinnaker Classes 1 & 2 FG 10.35

Please refer to SI’s 7.3, 7.3.1 and amended SI 7.3.2

All communications for all classes will be on the VHF Ch. 69 for these races. This includes

‘change of course’ or shortened course information.

Start Area – see individual course below

Starting Line: SI FB 2.1 applies

Finishing Line: SI FC 6 applies

COURSES:

Start areas and midway points

Course ALPHA (A): start area at mark C; midway point Mark E (bearing 090 degrees).

Course BRAVO (B): start area at mark C; midway point Black Tom (bearing 090 degrees).

Course CHARLIE (C): start area at mark J; midway point Black Tom (bearing 090 degrees).

Course DELTA (D): start area at mark J; midway point Black Tom (bearing 090 degrees).
